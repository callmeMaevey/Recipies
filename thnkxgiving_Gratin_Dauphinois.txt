5 lbs russet potatoes 
Parmesan (.75cup) and Gruyere (1.5 cups) (get good cheese)
fresh thyme
many cloves garlic
lots of salt
white pepper 
3 Shallots

I will be making this in a dutch oven, mostly for vibes. the walls might be too high through


cream mixture (to be made the day before):

    3 medium shallots, quartered through root ends
    3.25 cups heavy cream,
    1 Tbsp. salt,
    1 tsp. white pepper,
    1 Tbsp. thyme leaves,
    4 garlic cloves 
    
    Simmer in a small saucepan over low heat; careful not to curdle
    cook until shallots and garlic are very soft, (crush easily)
    like 15 minutes. 
    let cool a little, then immersion blend.
    store in refrigerator overnight

Baking dish:
preheat oven to 400f
    rub raw cut garlic on base and walls of baking dish
    butter the dish with like 2 tbsp

    warm cream mixture slightly stirring to prevent burn - transfer to bowl
    peel potato, then mandoline each. attempting to minimize oxidation
        peel -> mandoline -> into cream
    mandoline potatoes to ~ 1/8th inch straight into cream mixture

    arrange potatoes so each is upright in baking dish, like ratatouille.
    salt and white pepper the cream mixture to taste
    add cream mixture (and some butter dots on top if youd like)
    cream mixture should be just above halfway on the potatoes
    but on heat to bring to soft simmer (very gentle) when edges bubbly move on
    cover and transfer to oven, for 30 minutes (till potatoes completely soft, ie. easily pierced)
    uncover for 15 minutes, in order to crisp and brown
    take out and cover in cheeses, then bake uncovered until deeply golden (15 mins?)

    rest for like 10 mins before serving
