cream mixture (to be made the day before):

    2 medium shallots, quartered through root ends
    2½ cups heavy cream,
    1 Tbsp. Diamond Crystal or 1¾ tsp. Morton kosher salt,
    1 tsp. freshly ground black pepper,
    1 Tbsp. thyme leaves,
    4 garlic cloves 
    
    Simmer in a small saucepan over low heat; careful not to curdle
    cook until shallots and garlic are very soft, (crush easily)
    like 15 minutes. 
    let cool a little, then emersion blend.
    store in refridgerator overnight

Baking dish:
preheat oven to 400f
    warm cream mixture slightly stirring to prevent burn - transfer to bowl
    peel potato, then mandoline each. attempting to minimize oxidation
        peel -> mandoline -> into cream
    mandoline potatoes to ~ 1/8th inch straight into cream mixture

    arrange potatoes so each is upright in baking dish, like ratatouille.
    add cream mixture
    but on heat to bring to soft simmer (very gentle) when edges bubbly move on
    cover and transfer to oven, for 30 minutes (till potatoes completely soft)
    take out and cover in cheeses, then bake uncovered until deeply golden (30 mins?)
