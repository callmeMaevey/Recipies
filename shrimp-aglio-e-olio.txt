start boiling a pot of water for pasta

slice garlic, not mince
mince shallot

heat pan and add a good amount of oil
add garlic + shallot + red pepper flakes
garlic should be starting to tan

pat shrimp dry, salt, then pat down again
add to pan and turn up heat
add white pepper
goal is to sear shrimp, then remove

add mushrooms, cook out their water briefly

add splash of wine and reduce by half
slowly add warmed butter, should combine with oil and wine

add undercooked pasta to pan, finish in oil/butter/garlic sauce
(I am adding spinach here for the macros, letting it cook in the sauce just long enough to wilt)

add shrimp back in, add 1 quarter squeeze of lemon and the zest of that wedge. add chopped parsley as well.

toss, take off heat, and add Parmesan on top
